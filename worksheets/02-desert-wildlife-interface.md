# System Design — Desert Urban Wildlife & Hobby-Farm Interface

A completed instance of the design worksheet, focused on the
desert urban-wildlife interface and hobby-farm operations.

## 1. Purpose of the system
- Detect unusual changes in wildlife and domestic animals that may signal
  disease emergence.

## 2. Target population
- Desert urban wildlife observers and hobby farmers at the urban-wildlife
  interface.

## 3. What will you give back? (bi-directionality)
- Conservation and prevention resources.
- Education brochures.
- Communication channels.
- Connection to human / animal disease resources, diagnostics, veterinary
  care, and human health services.
- Improved food security.
- Adherence guidance for policy makers.
- Protection of animal health and livelihoods.

## 4. Parameter changes vs. the Minimum Dataset
**Keep / emphasize:**
- Clinical signs in animals.
- Purpose of keeping animals.
- Coding for hunting, consuming, predation, pets.
- Animal interactions.

**Add:**
- Travel history.
- Animal / water turbidity.
- Herd / flock size.
- Food setup of location.
- Wildlife interaction (predation, baiting).

**Remove:**
- Vector-related questions deemed out of scope for this population.

## 5. Ensuring access (barriers, literacy, awareness)
- Social media.
- 1-800 text-message line.
- Phone-survey option.
- Language translation.
- Extension outreach.
- Food drives.
- Mailed materials.
- Tribal newspapers.
- Community boards.

## 6. Frequency of reporting
- Bi-monthly (low burden).

## 7. Time per report
- 5–7 minutes, depending on the report.

## 8. Key partners
- State extension / department of agriculture.
- Clinics.
- Food stores.
- Pet owners.
- Local farmers / ranchers (cattle, pigs, livestock).
- Local public-health department.
- Schools, organizations.
- Veterinarians.
- Tribes / Indigenous Nations.
- Forest Service.
- Social-media groups.

## 9. Validation / verification
- Local public-health on alert.
- Geographical validation.
- Phone calls / site visits.
- Verifying photos / documentation.
- Security review.

## 10. Elevator pitch
> We are designing an early-warning system to detect unusual diseases that
> can affect people. Our system will connect farmers and veterinarians with
> human and animal health experts to enhance the health and wellness of
> families and animals. Join us in keeping your communities safe.
