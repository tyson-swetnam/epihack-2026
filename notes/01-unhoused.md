# Unhoused — focus-area brainstorm

Hand-written breakout note exploring participatory-surveillance ideas for
people experiencing homelessness in Maricopa County.

## Ideas

- **Healthy Companions Program**
  - Vaccination of pets.
  - Healthy owners ↔ healthy pets.
  - Check for cell-phone availability — helps get participants into
    public-housing pipelines.

- **Heat Surveillance**
  - Resource-allocation alignment.
  - Maricopa County as the initial geography.

- **Shelter App**
  - Location-based.
  - Surfaces shelter availability / services.

## Observation

> People share their health issues with **public health** more often than
> with family or friends — anonymity matters.

This suggests the participatory channel should prioritize:

- Anonymous or pseudonymous reporting.
- Trusted intermediary (a public-health-branded channel rather than a
  social one).
- Privacy-preserving aggregation before any data is shared back.
