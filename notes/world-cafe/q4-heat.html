World Café Q4 — Heat

Prompt: What is an example of directly engaging with the public that was really successful?

Examples

  • Community Event → outreach. Use community events as the on-ramp for outreach activities.
  • Train the Trainer → Western Regional Public Health (HHS Region 9), a 12-year-old program. A long-running (12 years) train-the-trainer program in HHS Region 9.
  • Speak to media → viral or national syndication. A single well-placed media appearance can scale through national syndication.
  • Disseminate through other networks, e.g. FFA. Lean on existing member-based networks (Future Farmers of America was the example given) rather than building new ones.
  • Canvassing → door-to-door, last year’s data. Targeted door-to-door canvassing informed by the prior year’s surveillance data.
  • Community Health Workers → assess heat risk to homes. CHWs perform in-home heat-risk assessments.
Source: Ending Pandemics Academy · University of Arizona Mel & Enid Zuckerman College of Public Health · Global Health Institute.
