# World Café Q4 — Information Flow

**Prompt:** *What is an example of directly engaging with the public that was
really successful?*

## Examples

- **Go to them in person (face-to-face).** Physical presence is still the
  highest-bandwidth engagement channel.
- **Leverage cell-phone network to send reminders.** SMS reminders riding
  the existing mobile network.
- **Engaged with Board of Supervisors.** County-level political engagement
  unlocks downstream channels.
- **Emergency health alerts → geolocation.** Geo-targeted emergency health
  alerts.
- **EMR (Epic) with TMC.** Embed messaging in the electronic medical record
  — Epic at Tucson Medical Center as the example.
- **Dating apps → STI services (??).** Partnership between dating apps and
  STI testing/treatment services (noted as exploratory; the "??" in the
  original card flags uncertainty).
