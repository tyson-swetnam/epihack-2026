# World Café Q4 — Heat

**Prompt:** *What is an example of directly engaging with the public that was
really successful?*

## Examples

- **Community Event → outreach.** Use community events as the on-ramp for
  outreach activities.
- **Train the Trainer → Western Regional Public Health, 12 years old
  (Region 9).** A long-running train-the-trainer program in HHS Region 9.
- **Speak to media → viral or national syndication.** A single well-placed
  media appearance can scale through national syndication.
- **Disseminate through other networks, e.g. FFA.** Lean on existing
  member-based networks (Future Farmers of America was the example given)
  rather than building new ones.
- **Canvassing → door-to-door, last year's data.** Targeted door-to-door
  canvassing informed by the prior year's surveillance data.
- **Community Health Workers → assess heat risk to homes.** CHWs perform
  in-home heat-risk assessments.
