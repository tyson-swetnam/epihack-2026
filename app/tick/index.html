Welcome Step 1 of 6

You found a tick. Now what?

Mail the tick to the Great Arizona Tick Check lab. They'll identify the species and test it for the pathogens it can carry. In the meantime, we'll set you up with a 14-day symptom watchlist.

What we'll ask you for, in order:

  1. Where and when you found it (GPS, date, hours attached, body location).
  2. A photo of the tick (use the back camera if you can).
  3. Any symptoms you already have (skip this if none — most people will).
  4. What we do and don't keep from your report.

Takes about 3 minutes. You can stop at any step — nothing is saved until you tap Submit.

Where and when did you find it?

Powers the General + Exposure parts of the Minimum Dataset.

Tap “Use my location” or enter a ZIP below.

Photo of the tick

If you can, put it next to a coin or a fingertip for scale. The lab still needs the physical tick, but the photo helps us give you a first-pass species estimate now.

A photo is optional but strongly recommended. You can submit without one.

Any symptoms yet?

If you feel fine, skip this — that's the most common case. If anything starts up in the next two weeks, we'll remind you what to watch for on the next screen.

Skipping is fine. One tap of Next moves on.
