Heat check-in — coming in Phase 1

Not built yet

The Heat vertical lands in Phase 1, after the Phase 0 tick mail-in flow proves the agent chain end-to-end.

What Phase 1 will ship

  • CHW heat check-in — field-app flow for outreach workers with unsheltered residents (Scenario C in 04-data-flows).
  • Anonymous heat self-report — minimum-friction flow for community members.
  • "Where can I cool off?" — queries mag-hrn-mcp + 211-az-mcp for an open cooling center and on-demand transport.

Why it's behind tick mail-in

The Heat vertical depends on three MCPs that aren't shipped yet: nws-heatrisk-mcp, mag-hrn-mcp, adhs-mcp. Building those is the gating work for Phase 1; see roadmap.
