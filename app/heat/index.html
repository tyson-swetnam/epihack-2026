CHW check-in, anonymous self-report, and a cooling-center lookup — wired to the same agent pipeline as the tick mail-in.

CHW heat check-in

Six-step field flow for outreach workers with a tablet. Computes the heat-vulnerability score, finds the nearest open cooling center, and offers one-tap transport via 211 Arizona.

Heat self-report

Five-step anonymous flow. Same vulnerability scoring as the CHW flow, but offers a phone link to 211 instead of dispatching a vehicle.
