AZ One Health Sentinel

What did you see today?

A two-vertical participatory surveillance prototype: tick mail-in (Phase 0) plus three heat-vertical flows (Phase 1).

Submit a tick

Mail in a tick for species identification and get a 14-day symptom watchlist. Takes about 3 minutes on a phone.

Heat check-in (CHW)

Six-step field flow for community health workers. Computes the heat-vulnerability score, finds the nearest open cooling center, and offers one-tap transport via 211 Arizona.

Phase 1 Heat Scenario C Works offline

Heat self-report

Five-step anonymous flow for community members. Same vulnerability scoring as the CHW flow; offers a phone link to 211 instead of dispatching a vehicle.
