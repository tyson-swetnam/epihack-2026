## What we wanted

A working knowledge framework for participatory One Health surveillance
in Arizona — anchored on the ten-prompt design worksheet
(Figure 5) and the eight-class minimum dataset (Figure 2).

## What we built

- **Five reference figures** under `figures/index.html` — purpose,
  minimum dataset, timeliness metrics, design lifecycle, design worksheet.
- **Two focus groups** under `wildlife/` and `heat/` with 30+ anchor
  resources each, spanning state, county, tribal, federal, academic, and
  citizen-science jurisdictions.
- **Two completed design worksheets** under `worksheets/` — unusual
  animal-health events and the desert urban-wildlife interface.
- **World Café Q4 notes** capturing the public-engagement tactics that
  participants kept returning to (CHW heat-risk checks, train-the-trainer
  in Region 9, dating apps ↔ STI flow, the shelter app).
