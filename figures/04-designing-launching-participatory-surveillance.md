# Designing & Launching Participatory Surveillance

A 12-step lifecycle for designing, launching, and sustaining a participatory
surveillance system.

## Steps

| # | Step | Phase |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Assess Needs of Community** | Design |
| 2 | **Determine Purpose of System** | Design |
| 3 | **Identify Stakeholders** | Design |
| 4 | **Ascertain Resources** | Design |
| 5 | **Co-Design System** | Design |
| 6 | **Test Prototypes & Finalize Product** | Build |
| 7 | **Launch the System** | Launch |
| 8 | **Validate Data** | Operate |
| 9 | **Create Awareness Campaigns** | Operate |
| 10 | **Train Recruiters** | Operate |
| 11 | **Incentivize & Retain Users** | Operate |
| 12 | **Monitor, Evaluate & Adapt System** | Operate |
