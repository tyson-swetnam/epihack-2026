# Designing a One Health Participatory Surveillance System (Worksheet)

A breakout-session worksheet used at EpiHack Arizona to apply the
[12-step design lifecycle](./04-designing-launching-participatory-surveillance.md)
to a concrete focus area. Each team fills in the answers; completed
worksheets become candidate system designs.

## Template prompts

1. **What is the purpose of your system?**
2. **Define the target population(s)?**
3. **What will you be giving back to the target population? (i.e. bi-directionality)**
4. **Which parameters of the Minimum Dataset for OH Participatory Surveillance
   would you remove and/or which additional parameters would you include?**
5. **How will you ensure that the target population has access to this system
   (e.g. technological barriers, literacy, awareness)?**
6. **How frequently will you ask participants to report?**
7. **How long do you expect a report to take the participant (on average)?**
8. **Who are key partners to develop, implement, promote adoption, and
   maintain this system?**
9. **How will you validate or verify the data?**
10. **Describe this system in 3-5 sentences for key stakeholders
    (i.e., your elevator pitch).**
