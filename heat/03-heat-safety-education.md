# Q3 — Heat-safety education

> **What do you provide for education on severe heat and what to do?**

## Education already produced for Arizona

### State-level
- **ADHS Heat Safety / Heat Preparedness** materials:
  <https://www.azdhs.gov/preparedness/epidemiology-disease-control/extreme-weather/heat-safety/heat-preparedness/index.php>
- **ADHS Climate & Health Program** — established 2013 under the CDC
  BRACE cooperative agreement; produces climate-and-health profile,
  vulnerability assessments, intervention assessments, and adaptation
  plans.

### Regional / county
- **MAG Heat Relief Network** ([azmag.gov/Programs/Heat-Relief-Network](https://azmag.gov/Programs/Heat-Relief-Network)).
- **Maricopa County Public Health** — annual heat-mortality reports,
  Fight the Bite-style seasonal campaigns,
  [Clear Channel digital-billboard campaign](https://www.maricopa.gov/m/newsflash/Home/Detail/3662).
- **Pima County — Stay Informed During Extreme Heat:**
  <https://www.pima.gov/3225/Stay-Informed-Extreme-Heat>.
- **City of Phoenix Office of Heat Response and Mitigation** —
  HeatReadyPHX materials, tree-and-shade master plans, cool-pavement
  pilots, reflective bus-shelter roof studies.

### Federal
- **NWS HeatRisk** color-coded daily forecast at the ZIP-code level
  (Green / Yellow / Orange / Red / Magenta).
- **CDC Extreme Heat** topic pages with translated materials.
- **NIHHIS** (NOAA's National Integrated Heat Health Information
  System) — federal coordination on heat health.
- **CDC / ATSDR Place and Health — Extreme Heat Adaptation:**
  <https://www.atsdr.cdc.gov/place-health/share/extreme-heat-adaptation.html>.

### Academic / research-to-practice
- **UA Heat Resilience Initiative** ([heat.arizona.edu](https://heat.arizona.edu/))
  — umbrella organization that coordinates DOE SW-IFL, NIHHIS Center
  for Heat Resilient Communities, NOAA CLIMAS (rural / border / tribal
  heat focus), CDC BRACE, and NIH SCORCH.
- **AZ Climate & Health (UA)** — patient-facing material at
  <https://azclimatehealth.arizona.edu/resources/patients>.
- **ASU Knowledge Exchange for Resilience** — co-leads the Arizona
  Heat Resilience Workgroup; informed the Governor's Extreme Heat
  Preparedness Plan.

## What the World Café cards already surfaced

From the [Heat Q4 card](../notes/world-cafe/q4-heat.md), examples of
successful direct public engagement on heat in Arizona:

- **Community Health Workers (CHWs)** doing in-home heat-risk
  assessments.
- **Train-the-Trainer** through Western Regional Public Health
  (HHS Region 9), a 12-year-old program.
- **Targeted door-to-door canvassing** informed by the prior year's
  surveillance data.
- **Media syndication** of a single well-placed public-health
  appearance.
- **Existing membership networks** (FFA was the example) as
  dissemination channels.
- **Community events** as the on-ramp to outreach.

## Gaps the team could target

- **Indigenous-language** and **plain-language** material gaps,
  especially for older adults and outdoor workers.
- **Just-in-time** push messaging tied to NWS HeatRisk overrides
  (Red / Magenta days) rather than seasonal campaigns alone.
- **Occupational** heat-safety material for landscape, construction,
  agriculture, and food-delivery workers.
- **Pet** and **service-animal** guidance integrated with
  cooling-center hours.
- **Schools** — back-to-school in AZ overlaps with peak heat. K-12
  curriculum integration is uneven.
