Heat

EpiHack Arizona 2026 focus group — surveillance, response, and participatory systems for extreme heat in Arizona, the hottest large metropolitan region in the United States.

The scale of the problem

4,320+heat-exposure deaths in AZ, 2013–2024
990heat-related deaths in 2023 (state total)
~4,298heat-related ER visits per year (AZ)
2005Maricopa heat surveillance started
200+cooling / hydration sites on the MAG Heat Relief Network
36%of 2016 Maricopa heat deaths among unsheltered residents

The four guiding questions

1How do you inform the public about cooling-center locations? MAG HRN, ADHS Heat Preparedness Network ArcGIS map, Pima County map, UA CRH map, 211 Arizona. 2Do cooling centers share resources in real time? Mostly no — current gaps, data-model sketch, and technology options. 3What do you provide for heat-safety education? ADHS BRACE, UA Heat Resilience Initiative, NWS HeatRisk, CHW outreach, FFA-style networks. 4Who is most vulnerable to heat in AZ? Unsheltered residents, older adults, AI/AN and Black Maricopa residents, outdoor workers, tribal rural communities.

State of Arizona

ADHS Heat Safety / Climate & Health Program state · public-health authority

Heat-preparedness materials and the statewide ArcGIS Experience map of Heat Preparedness Network locations. ADHS Climate & Health Program was established in 2013 under the CDC BRACE cooperative agreement. Publishes the annual heat-mortality report (990 deaths in 2023; >4,320 since 2013).

ADHS Heat Preparedness · Heat Preparedness Network map · Mortality report portal

Counties and cities

Maricopa County (MCDPH) — Heat Surveillance county

Heat-associated mortality surveillance since 2005/2006. Real-time updates during the season, annual Heat-Associated Deaths in Maricopa County report; surfaced the unsheltered burden (36% of 2016 deaths) and the AI/AN and Black mortality disparities.

maricopa.gov / Heat Surveillance

Maricopa Association of Governments (MAG) — Heat Relief Network regional

200+ cooling, hydration, respite, and donation sites; May 1 – September 30; pet-friendly filter; running since 2005.

hrn.azmag.gov · MAG program page

City of Phoenix — Office of Heat Response and Mitigation city

First publicly-funded municipal heat office in the U.S. (2021), directed by Dr. David Hondula (ASU). Runs HeatReadyPHX, tree-and-shade master plans, cool-pavement pilots, and reflective bus-shelter roof studies.

phoenix.gov/heat

Pima County — Cooling Centers county

~3 dozen air-conditioned cooling centers and hydration stations stocked with cooling towels, water, electrolyte packets, and education materials.

pima.gov / Cooling Centers · Stay Informed

Tribal

CLIMAS · Inter Tribal Council of Arizona TEC tribal · academic partnership

CLIMAS (NOAA-funded since 1998) is a UA + ITCA + NMSU partnership explicitly working on rural, border, and tribal heat resilience. ITCA-TEC builds tribally-driven epidemiologic capacity across the Phoenix and Tucson IHS Areas.

climas.arizona.edu · ITCA-TEC

Federal

CDC BRACE · NSSP / BioSense · ATSDR Place & Health federal

Climate-Ready States & Cities Initiative (BRACE) — AZ is one of 16 states / 2 cities. AZ ED heat-illness data flows through the National Syndromic Surveillance Program (BioSense). ATSDR's Place and Health program publishes Extreme Heat Adaptation guidance. 2025 MMWR reported on cooling-center awareness, use, and access barriers in Maricopa County.

MMWR 2025 — Maricopa cooling centers · ATSDR Heat Adaptation

NOAA NWS — HeatRisk · NIHHIS federal

NWS Phoenix and Tucson issue daily ZIP-code-level HeatRisk forecasts (Green / Yellow / Orange / Red / Magenta) that trigger Extreme Heat Watch and Warning products. NIHHIS coordinates federal heat health work across NOAA + HHS and funds the UA-led Center for Heat Resilient Communities.

NWS Phoenix Heat · NWS Tucson

Academic and research

University of Arizona — Heat Resilience Initiative academic · umbrella

Coordinates DOE-funded SW-IFL, NIHHIS-funded Center for Heat Resilient Communities, NOAA CLIMAS, CDC BRACE, and NIH SCORCH. MEZCOPH received $2M from CDC for AZ heat interventions.

heat.arizona.edu · AZ Climate & Health (patient resources) · UA CRH AZ Cooling Centers Map

ASU — Knowledge Exchange for Resilience academic

Co-leads the Arizona Heat Resilience Workgroup; informed the Governor's Extreme Heat Preparedness Plan. Heat researchers including Dr. David Hondula (also Phoenix OHRM director) lead urban-heat-island work.

resilience.asu.edu · Healthy Urban Environments

Participatory and public-facing

211 Arizona · CHWs · Train-the-Trainer participatory

211 Arizona phone routing in English and Spanish for cooling centers, transportation, utility assistance, and emergency AC repair. Community Health Workers do in-home heat-risk assessments. The 12-year-old Western Regional Public Health Train-the-Trainer program (HHS Region 9) was named in the World Café Q4 Heat card as a proven scale channel.

211arizona.org / Heat Relief
